Each location is a separate Saskatoon return trip. Enter total return kilometres, including local driving.

Commercial $130 is the coordinated volume rate, not necessarily the rate for a standalone smaller job. Tax fields are illustrative; confirm what is taxable.

Service locations

Each location can include its own materials. Enter the quantity and your unit cost, then choose a markup percentage. Materials are added to the suggested quote, and their purchase cost is deducted from project contribution.

Door inspection sheets

One checklist per door, linked to the project. Save before changing projects. Export reports for backup.

Project estimate

Quotes are editable customer prices before sales tax. Business contribution is before general overhead and income tax. The vehicle allowance already covers vehicle operating costs; do not deduct fuel again.
